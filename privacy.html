Privacy Policy – Heritage of the Kurds

Heritage of the Kurds respects your privacy.

The application does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, or payment information.

The app uses Google Firebase services to support its technical infrastructure and to provide application content. Firebase may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and maintain its services.

Heritage of the Kurds does not display advertisements and does not sell users' personal information to third parties.

The application is intended to provide educational and informational content related to Kurdish history, culture, and heritage.
